Your local roofer in Chipping Norton

Roof Centre provides professional repairs for all types of roofs, from broken tiles to rusted sheets. Whether your leak was caused by weather, rust, debris or overflowing water, our roofers will find the problem and fix it.

Chipping Norton is about 27 kilometres south-west of the Sydney CBD, in the City of Liverpool and the South Western Sydney region.

Ageing tile roofs

Many homes in the area have original concrete or terracotta tile roofs. Over time the tiles become porous, the ridge pointing cracks and valley irons rust. Depending on the overall condition, these roofs can be repaired, restored or replaced with metal.

Storm season

Summer storms in Western Sydney can bring hail, strong winds and heavy rain. If your roof is damaged, see our emergency make-safe page for what to do.

Common roof problems we fix

  • Leaking roofs: often caused by cracked tiles, failed flashings or rusted valleys.
  • Broken or slipped tiles: high winds and storms can crack or move tiles and ridge capping.
  • Rusted metal roofs: rusted sheets, screws and flashings let water in over time.
  • Moss and lichen: holds moisture against the roof and wears down tiles over time.
  • Blocked or damaged gutters: can push water back under the roof and into the eaves.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Contact our team for a free quote or more information. Call 0431 848 619 or send us an enquiry.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Roof Centre is licensed and fully insured, and we can provide builder and client references on request.

How do I know if my roof needs repair or replacement?

If the damage is limited to one area, such as a few broken tiles or a leaking flashing, a repair is usually enough. If the roof material is failing across the whole roof, or leaks keep coming back, replacement may be better value. We will inspect your roof and explain the options.

My roof is leaking during a storm. What should I do?

Stay off the roof. Move belongings away from the leak, put out buckets and keep people out of any room where the ceiling is sagging. Call us to arrange a make-safe. For storm emergencies where life or property is at immediate risk, call the NSW SES on 132 500, or 000 in a life-threatening emergency.
