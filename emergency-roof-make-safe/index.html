Emergency Roof Make Safe

When a storm, fallen branch or sudden failure leaves your roof open to the weather, a make-safe stops further damage until permanent repairs can be done.

Request a Quote Call 0431 848 619

What a make-safe involves

A make-safe is temporary work to protect your home. Depending on the damage, it can include:

  • Tarping damaged sections of the roof and securing the tarp
  • Temporarily replacing or refixing displaced tiles and sheets
  • Securing loose sheets, flashings or capping that could blow off
  • Clearing debris that is blocking water from draining

Once the roof is secure, we can inspect the damage properly and quote for permanent repairs or replacement.

What to do while you wait

  • Stay off the roof, especially in wet or windy weather
  • Keep clear of fallen power lines and call your electricity network if lines are down
  • Move furniture and valuables away from leaks and put out buckets
  • If a ceiling is sagging with water, keep everyone out of that room
  • Take photos of the damage for your insurer

For storm or flood emergencies where life or property is at immediate risk, contact the NSW SES on 132 500, or call 000 in a life-threatening emergency.

Insurance

If you plan to make an insurance claim, contact your insurer as early as possible. Photos taken before and after the make-safe will help with your claim.

Signs you may need this

  • Storm or hail damage
  • Fallen trees or branches
  • Lifted or missing roof sheets
  • Water coming through the ceiling
  • Tiles blown off or displaced

Questions about emergency roof make safe

Is a make-safe a permanent repair?

No. A make-safe is temporary protection to stop further damage. Permanent repairs are quoted and carried out once the roof is secure and conditions are safe.
